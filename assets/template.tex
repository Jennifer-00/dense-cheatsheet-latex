\G{Kinetic energy} $E_k=\tfrac12mv^2$ (J). \R{Trap:} $v$ in m/s, not km/h.\\
{\setlength{\tabcolsep}{1pt}\begin{tabular}{|Y{10mm}|Y{14mm}|Y{22mm}|}
\hline\rowcolor{cb!12}\B{Symbol}&\B{English}&\B{Meaning}\\\hline
$m$&mass&in kg\\\hline
$E_k$&kinetic energy&energy of motion\\\hline
\end{tabular}}
\par\Eg{Example 1} Given $m=2$ kg, $v=3$ m/s. Find $E_k$.
Solution: $E_k=\tfrac12mv^2$ $=\tfrac12\cdot2\cdot3^2$ $=9$ J.
\par\why{Explain why doubling the speed quadruples the kinetic energy.}{$E_k\propto v^2$ (平方关系, quadratic dependence)。}
